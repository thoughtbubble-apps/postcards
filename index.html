How to make a digital postcard

PostcardPop is a browser-based digital postcard maker. Choose a photo by pasting its image URL, then turn the card over to write a note. Add the recipient and sender names and, if you like, enter a city or place for the location stamp. PostcardPop creates a share link that the people you send it to can open in a web browser.

  1. Choose a photo. Paste the image URL into the field on the front of the card. The card preview updates with that image.
  2. Write the back. Tap or click the card to turn it over. Add your message, recipient, sender, and an optional location.
  3. Copy and share. Use the Copy Link button, then send that link through the email or messaging service you already use.

A postcard that opens in a browser

PostcardPop prepares a digital card; it does not print a paper postcard or send the link for you. Anyone with the link can open and read the postcard because the link contains its details. Recipients can view the photo and flip the card to read your note.

The location stamp uses map tiles from OpenStreetMap contributors .
